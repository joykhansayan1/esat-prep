Restore purchases

On the paywall or in Settings, tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account (or, on Android, Google account) used to buy. Purchases are handled by Apple StoreKit on iPhone and iPad and by Google Play Billing on Android.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or via apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

On Android, open the Google Play app → tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ESAT Prep → Cancel subscription, or visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone, iPad) or through Google Play (Android).
